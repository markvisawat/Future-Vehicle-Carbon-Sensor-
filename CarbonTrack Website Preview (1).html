Introducing CarbonTrack

A small sensor.
A bigger picture.

CarbonTrack is a proposed automotive CO₂ exhaust sensor and data platform. The vision is to collect vehicle emissions information and present it in a clear, useful dashboard.

  • Explore continuous exhaust monitoring
  • Review trip and longer-term trends
  • Make complex readings easier to understand
  • Support future fleet and vehicle research
The concept flow

From exhaust to insight.

A high-level view of how the proposed system could work, subject to technical validation.

STEP 01

Measure

A suitable sensor samples exhaust gas and captures relevant readings.

STEP 02

Transfer

Readings are sent to a connected device or data logger.

STEP 03

Understand

A dashboard turns recorded data into trends and summaries.

STEP 04

Improve

Insights may inform research, fleet analysis and future design decisions.
